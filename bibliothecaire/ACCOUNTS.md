# Account inventory

Fill by hand — this needs no email access and is the prerequisite for
every design lane (see README.md). One row per account; `stakes` drives
the triage order in `triage.py`.

Columns:
- **address** — the account
- **provider** — gmail / outlook
- **purpose** — what this account is for, in your own words
- **volume** — rough inbound/day (guess is fine)
- **stakes** — high / medium / low: how bad is a missed message here?
- **pain** — what currently goes wrong (unread pileup, missed replies,
  spam, "only exists for one service", …)
